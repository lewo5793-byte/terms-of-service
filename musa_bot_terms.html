2. Purpose of the Bot

The Bot connects Roblox group membership with Discord server roles. Its functions may include checking whether a Discord user has linked a Roblox account through RoWifi, checking membership of specified MUSA Roblox groups, assigning the corresponding Discord role, and updating roles when group membership changes. The Bot supports MUSA community administration and role management.
